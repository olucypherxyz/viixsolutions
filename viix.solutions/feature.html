Engagement Framework

How a Managed-Services Engagement Works

VIIX works on a retainer basis with a defined monthly support allocation, clear response expectations, and structured reporting. Clients choose the service level that matches current needs — Essential, Business, or Enterprise — and the partnership can scale as the business grows. Work outside the agreed managed scope is quoted and approved through a change request or statement of work before it begins.
