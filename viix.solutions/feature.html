How a Managed-Services Engagement Works

VIIX works on a retainer basis with a defined monthly support allocation, clear response expectations, and structured reporting. Clients choose the service level that matches current needs — Essential, Business, or Enterprise — and the partnership can scale as the business grows. Work outside the agreed managed scope is quoted and approved through a change request or statement of work before it begins.

1

Discovery

We learn your business, priorities, and operating environment.

2

Assessment

We evaluate current systems, gaps, and risks before recommending a service level.

3

Service Planning

A scoped managed-services plan with clear allocation and reporting rhythm.

4

Managed Delivery

Ongoing support and project delivery under the agreed relationship.

5

Reporting & Escalation

Structured reporting and clear escalation paths — not an informal cellphone relationship.

6

Scale & Improve

Add domains, increase allocation, or upgrade tiers as the business grows — without starting from scratch.
