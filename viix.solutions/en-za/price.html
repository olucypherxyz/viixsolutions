Scoped to Your Risk — Not a Public Price List

We don't publish a price list — because the right package depends on your infrastructure, your headcount, your risk exposure, and where you already are. A number without that context isn't useful to you or to us.

What we can tell you upfront:
Managed IT is structured across service levels — Essential, Business, and Enterprise — so support capacity can match where your business is today and scale as you grow.
Engagements use a defined monthly allocation, clear response expectations, and structured reporting. Work outside the agreed managed scope is quoted and approved before it begins.
Cybersecurity support, vulnerability assessment, and practical security controls can be included where appropriate — scoped to your environment, not promised as continuous monitoring or a security operations centre.
Brand and media work is typically scoped separately once your IT foundation is stable.
