How We Work With You

Stabilise the Foundation First

VIIX provides managed IT and IT support for South African SMEs — including businesses in and around Cape Town — who need an accountable alternative to informal freelance support. Through an ongoing managed-services relationship we cover day-to-day helpdesk, systems and network administration, endpoint and device support, Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace administration, backup and recovery, and security-conscious operations designed to reduce downtime and POPIA-related exposure. Technology advisory and structured reporting are part of how the relationship is managed — not a one-off ticket response.
