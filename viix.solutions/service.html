What We Do

Five Service Pillars. Clear Engagement Paths.

VIIX Solutions provides implementation, technical operations, managed services and creative execution. Engagements are scoped to need — not every client uses every pillar, and Managed Services tiers do not automatically include every service.

01

Managed IT

Helpdesk-style support, cloud administration and security-conscious operations for businesses that need dependable day-to-day technology coverage.

02

Web & Digital Infrastructure

Websites and digital foundations that improve online presence and give customers a clear, reliable way to find and engage you.

03

Business Systems & Automation

CRM, workflow and automation work that reduces manual effort and connects how teams operate.

04

Media & Creative Execution

Brand, content and creative assets produced for practical commercial use and ongoing management where needed.

05

Deployment & Technical Projects

Migrations, implementations and technical projects delivered with clear ownership from scoping through handover.

ICT

Managed IT, infrastructure & technical projects

Managed IT support, infrastructure, cloud administration, device management, security-conscious operations and scoped technical projects under a clear delivery relationship.

Media

Web presence & creative execution

Websites, brand communications, content and creative production managed for consistency — not only as one-off bursts.

Business

Systems, automation & operational support

Business systems, process support, CRM implementation and selected operational coordination — scoped to your engagement.

Regional sites lead where demand is strongest today — managed IT in Southern Africa; web and brand work in Nigeria — while the full service set remains available as you grow.
