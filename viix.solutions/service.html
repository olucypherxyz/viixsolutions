What We Do

Four capability domains. One managed-services partnership.

VIIX brings technology, operations, marketing, and customer management together under one relationship — so growing businesses are not left coordinating freelancers, agencies, and ad-hoc support on their own. Engagements are scoped to need; not every client uses every capability.

Technology & IT Services

Managed IT support, infrastructure, cloud administration, device management, and security-conscious operations under a structured service relationship.

Digital Marketing & Media

Brand communications, websites, content, campaigns, social media, SEO, and creative production managed consistently — not in bursts.

Business Operations

Operational support capabilities that can be brought into the same partnership when a business needs more than technology and marketing alone — scoped per engagement.

Customer Relations & Sales Support

CRM, customer-service, and sales-support capabilities that help turn interest into revenue when the business is ready to systematise those processes.

Regional sites focus acquisition where demand is strongest today — managed IT in Southern Africa; websites and branding in Nigeria — while the broader partnership remains available as you grow.
